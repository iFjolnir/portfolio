About me.

hello@fainaiasen.com

I'm Faye — a graphic & product designer, and a design educator working across brand identity, editorial, web, UI/UX and product design.

Before moving to Busan, I spent three years at De Montfort University Kazakhstan, where I taught graphic design, led two BA (Hons) programmes and went on to head the Art & Design department.

Now I design print and digital work as an international freelancer, study Korean full-time in preparation to enter a Master's programme, and build small, self-contained web tools along the way.

In my own work and in teaching, I care most about process: the thinking that connects concept, context and craft.
